# Eionet Thematic Group on Digital Capabilities and AI

Documentation from the Eionet Thematic Group on Digital Capabilities and AI
in-person meeting held at the European Environment Agency (EEA) in Copenhagen
on 29–30 September 2026.

## About the meeting

The meeting was the third meeting of the thematic group. It followed the group
survey and the work completed on 17 June 2026. Its purpose was to turn the
group's priority questions into a shared mission, measurable targets, and a
practical way to achieve them:

> How can we support each other in leveraging the potential of AI to maximize
> the potential of Eionet?

The meeting combined presentations, participant check-in and check-out
activities, four topic discussions, parallel working sessions, and a roadmap
workshop.

### Expected outputs

- A shared main aim and targets to be achieved by 31 December 2028.
- A portfolio of outcomes and actions across the four thematic topics.
- A roadmap with milestones addressing the group's priorities.
- A lightweight working model covering meetings, assignments, reporting, and
  communication.

## The four thematic topics

### T1 — Strategy, Governance and Organisation

The group explored organisational AI governance, compliance, ethics, the
division of responsibilities between the EU and Member States, and the
environmental footprint of data centres.

Key directions included:

- Developing governance checklists and guidance for AI-ready organisations.
- Collecting and evaluating national strategies, policies, and good practices.
- Supporting needs-led incubation that combines skills, technology, data, and
  ethics.
- Improving transparency of data-centre energy and water consumption.

### T2 — People, Skills and Ways of Working

This topic focused on AI literacy, roles and personas, learning needs, and
ways of helping organisations build an AI-literate and resilient workforce.

Key directions included:

- Defining AI roles, personas, skills, and learning journeys.
- Assessing AI literacy and capability needs across Eionet members.
- Developing guidance, tools, and training for different roles, including
  management and leadership.
- Exploring a common Eionet learning platform and training with
  Eionet-based certification.

### T3 — Data, Technologies and Infrastructure

The group discussed AI-ready environmental data, reusable infrastructure,
open-source technology, data standards, and secure access to platforms and
tools.

Key directions included:

- Defining criteria and guidance for AI-ready and reusable data.
- Creating reference architectures and an overview of technology and
  infrastructure options.
- Exploring an open AI hub for prototyping and development, including common
  hardware, APIs, knowledge bases, and platforms.
- Developing agentic capabilities and data connections such as the Model
  Context Protocol (MCP).
- Sharing best practices and supporting data sovereignty and appropriate access.

### T4 — AI Applications and Value Creation

This topic connected practical AI applications with environmental value,
adoption, efficiency, and responsible prioritisation.

Key directions included:

- Mapping AI applications, obstacles, patterns, and solutions.
- Prioritising tasks where AI assistance or automation can create clear value.
- Exploring energy-efficient and locally controlled AI solutions.
- Supporting environmental data collection, processing, metadata completion,
  analytics, and satellite-imagery use cases.
- Developing shared infrastructure and an Eionet AI space for prototyping.

## Meeting outcomes and working model

The discussions were structured around four questions for each topic:

1. What targets should the group pursue?
2. What outcomes would demonstrate progress?
3. What actions are needed?
4. Who can contribute, and how should the group take the work forward?

The group agreed that the thematic group should:

- **Lead** and facilitate discussions and prepare lightweight common outputs.
- **Support** relevant EEA and Eionet initiatives.
- **Connect** members with similar needs, experience, or use cases.
- **Monitor** relevant AI developments and share information with members.

The group is not intended to provide formal governance, mandatory
implementation, procurement, or operational system delivery.
